Use after research notes exist (or use `/vibe-prd`).

```text
You are drafting a short living PRD for HITSHCM.

Inputs: our research notes / MEMORY / answers in this chat.
Output: fill PRD.md sections - Vision, Problem, Users, Outcome, MVP In/Out,
```
